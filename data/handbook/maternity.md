# Maternity and newborn care

Prenatal visits, routine ultrasounds and lab tests during pregnancy are covered at 100 percent with no co-pay when the member uses a network provider.

The hospital stay for delivery is covered for up to 2 days after a vaginal birth and up to 4 days after a cesarean section. A longer stay needs approval from the plan's care team.

A newborn is covered from the day of birth, but the member must add the baby to the plan within 30 days of the birth. Babies added after 30 days start coverage on the first day of the next month.

The plan pays for one breast pump per pregnancy and up to 6 lactation consultant visits.
