# Claims and appeals

If the plan denies a claim or a request for prior authorization, the member receives a written notice explaining the reason and how to appeal.

A member has 180 days from the date of the denial notice to file an appeal. The plan decides standard appeals within 30 days.

If waiting could seriously harm the member's health, the member can ask for an expedited appeal, which is decided within 72 hours.

If the plan upholds the denial, the member can request an external review by an independent organization within 4 months of the final decision.
