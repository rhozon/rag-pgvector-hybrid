# Mental health and substance use

Outpatient therapy with a network therapist has a $20 co-pay per session, and there is no limit on the number of sessions when they are medically necessary.

Inpatient mental health and substance use treatment requires prior authorization, except in an emergency.

Members and their family can call the 24-hour crisis line at any time. The call is free and confidential.

The employee assistance program offers up to 5 free counseling sessions per issue, per year, without using the medical benefit.
