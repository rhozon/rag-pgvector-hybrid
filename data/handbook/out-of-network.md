# Out-of-network reimbursement

Care from providers outside the network is covered at 60 percent of the allowed amount after the out-of-network deductible of $1,000 per person.

To be reimbursed, the member submits an itemized bill and proof of payment through the member portal within 365 days of the date of service.

The plan pays reimbursements by direct deposit within 15 business days after it approves a complete claim.

Out-of-network providers may bill the member for the difference between their charge and the allowed amount, except for emergency care.
