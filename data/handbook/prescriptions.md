# Prescription drugs

The plan uses a three-tier formulary. Tier 1 covers generic drugs with a $10 co-pay for a 30-day supply. Tier 2 covers preferred brand drugs with a $35 co-pay. Tier 3 covers non-preferred brand drugs with a $70 co-pay.

Maintenance drugs taken every day, such as blood pressure or cholesterol medication, can be ordered by mail for a 90-day supply at two times the 30-day co-pay.

Specialty drugs, such as biologics, must be filled through the plan's specialty pharmacy and require prior authorization. The member pays 20 percent coinsurance, capped at $250 per fill.
