# Emergency care

Members can go to any emergency room, in or out of network, when they believe their life or health is at serious risk. Emergency visits never need prior authorization.

The emergency room co-pay is $150 per visit. The co-pay is waived if the member is admitted to the hospital within 24 hours of arriving at the emergency room.

Urgent care centers handle problems that need attention the same day but are not life threatening, such as sprains, minor cuts or a high fever. The urgent care co-pay is $40 per visit.

After an emergency stay at an out-of-network hospital, the member must call the plan within 48 hours so that care can be moved to a network facility once the member is stable.
