# Dental benefit

Two cleanings and two routine exams per calendar year are covered at 100 percent. X-rays are covered once every 12 months.

Fillings and simple extractions are covered at 80 percent after the dental deductible of $50. Crowns, bridges and root canals are covered at 50 percent.

The plan pays at most $1,500 in dental benefits per member per calendar year. Orthodontics is covered only for members under 19, with a lifetime maximum of $2,000.
