# Telehealth

Members can see a doctor by video or phone 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, through the plan's telehealth app.

The first 6 telehealth visits in each calendar year are free. After that, each visit costs $15.

Telehealth doctors can diagnose common conditions, give medical advice and send prescriptions to a pharmacy. They cannot prescribe controlled substances.

Virtual visits with a licensed therapist are also available through the app and follow the mental health benefit rules.
